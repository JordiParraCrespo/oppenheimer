Group 04

Overlays

The only layers allowed to cast a shadow.

Callout

neutral · info · success · warning · error

A note in the flow, not a card: flat tonal fill, hairline-free, 13px text. It never outweighs the form it sits above, and it never carries a button — if an action is needed, the action lives in the form. Neutral is the default and carries no hue — most notes are explanations, not state. The four tinted variants take a status hue at 11–14% behind full-opacity ink, and only when something actually is in that state.

Dialog · Destructive confirm

op-dialog · role=alertdialog · op-btn--danger

Deleting a session, removing a host, deleting a project. The title names the thing, the body states the cost in one or two sentences, and the button repeats the verb — never “Confirm”. Smaller confirmations (deleting a routine) stay inside the menu that asked.

Delete “Bump RN to 0.76”?

The session stops, its worktree is removed from the host, and the transcript is gone. Commits already pushed stay on the remote.

Delete this routine? Its triggers stop now. Past runs are kept.

scope chips · textarea · attach + permissions · model + effort · dictate · send
