The composer's chips are tighter. A bot's effort is named on its model chip ("GPT-6.2 Sol Low") and set from the slider at the foot of the model menu, as a session's is; the chip is as wide as the level it shows and holds it while the menu is open. The context meter is a ring, blue until 80% and amber after. Popovers take the 14px popover radius instead of the 28px modal one.
