In a WhatsApp group, a bot reads the last messages to work out who each new line is for, answers only what is meant for it (it is named, someone follows up on what it said, or asks it something), and keeps out of the rest, sending nothing: people's chat with each other, a remark about it to the others, a correction aimed at someone else, a thanks that needs nothing back. Every line still wakes it. No runner release is needed.
