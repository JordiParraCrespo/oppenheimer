Plan's To do is what you mean to work on, not only what is due: a task has a To do day (`focusDate`) apart from its due date, set in the task dialog, by the dashed row or with Add tasks. To do lists what is put on the day and what is due on it, keeps unfinished work from earlier days until it is done, and keeps a task ticked done on the day it was done.
