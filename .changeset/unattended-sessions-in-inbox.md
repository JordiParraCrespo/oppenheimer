A session whose finished turn has sat unread for thirty minutes is now an Inbox item that asks you to move it forward, in place of the browser notification that reminded about it. Its card takes a reply, typed into the agent's prompt, so the session goes back to work without leaving the Inbox. A check in the sessions sidebar's filter menu, Remind me after 30 minutes unread, on by default and kept on the device, turns it off.
