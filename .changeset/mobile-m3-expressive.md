Material 3 Expressive on Android. `MobileRoot` takes the brand `accent` and seeds every native control with it. New `SearchField` (Material's docked search bar on Android), `LoadingIndicator` (Expressive's wavy circular indicator; the system spinner on iOS) and `HeroAction` (an extended FAB on Android, a Liquid Glass pill on iOS). The Sessions and Employees searches, every spinner and an automation's Run now use them. `@oppenheimer/frontend-mobile` adds `TAB_BAR` and `withAlpha`, and the Android tab bar takes the brand blue in the app's scheme.
