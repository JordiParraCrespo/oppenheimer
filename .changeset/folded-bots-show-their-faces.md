A folded Bots heading keeps its bots' faces beside the label: each opens its chat and carries the state its row would (amber when it needs you, blue when unread, breathing while it works), and a `+N` chip unfolds the list when they do not all fit. The design system adds `BotFaces` and `BotFaceItem`, and `SidebarListHead` takes a `summary`.
