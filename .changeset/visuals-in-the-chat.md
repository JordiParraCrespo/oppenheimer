Visuals in the chat: an agent can show a titled card of typed blocks (figures, bar, line and area charts, a ring of shares, a ranking, a sortable table, a checklist, progress against targets, a record, a callout and links), drawn with the console's own charts in light and dark. Charts switch to their numbers as a table, figures count up, bars and rings grow in, and the whole card opens larger or copies its data. A spec that is not a visual shows a folded note with what is wrong instead of breaking the chat.
