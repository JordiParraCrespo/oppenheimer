Calendar: every entry on Day, Week and Month wears a colour, a wash of it with a bar down its left edge and its glyph in it: personal events blue, tasks violet, each Google account its own, automation runs in subtle ink. Free events wash lighter behind a dashed edge, all-day events deepest. The sidebar's layer glyphs take the same colours as a legend, and the first Google accounts now start on teal and green so they never share a colour with events or tasks. `CalendarLayerItem` takes `accent`; `CALENDAR_KIND_ACCENT` names the kinds' colours.
