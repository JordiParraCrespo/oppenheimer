Plan's sidebar views (Inbox, Tasks, Milestones, Calendar) can be dragged into the reader's own order, kept on this device, as the rail's items can; a click still opens the view, and Space picks one up from the keyboard. `SortableRoutineItem` is a `RoutineItem` that drags.
