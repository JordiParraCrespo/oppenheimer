Tasks board: drag from the board's empty space to draw a rectangle that picks every card it touches. ⌘, Ctrl or Shift held at the press adds to what is picked; a click on empty space clears it. Picked cards move together: drag one and the rest follow under it, or use Move to in the selection bar to send them all to a column's foot. `TaskMarquee` is the new rectangle.
