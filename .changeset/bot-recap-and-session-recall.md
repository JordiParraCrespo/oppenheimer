Catch up fast. Every bot has a second built-in skill, `recap`: run `/recap`, or `/recap <topic>`, or ask "where are we?", and it reads your sessions, pull requests, plan and the chat, then answers with where things stand, what waits on you and the next move. A coding agent in a session does the same when you ask it to recall or catch you up, from the note the runner writes beside its checkout.
