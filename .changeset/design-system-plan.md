`SortableItem`, `useSortableGroups`, `DropSlot`, `dragIgnore`) with the
frames' motion and keyboard support built in; `TaskBoard`, `TaskColumn`,

`DatePicker`; `CalendarLayerItem` and `CalendarSourceCard`;
`SessionPaneHeader` and `SessionPaneBack`; `PageHeaderDisplay` and
`PageHeaderStat`. The rail's doc names Plan as its third area.
