A bot's routine can run on a custom schedule: a five-field cron expression the bot writes from your words, for what once, hourly, daily, weekdays, weekly and monthly cannot say (several times a day, every 15 minutes during work hours, the last weekday of the month with `LW`, the second Tuesday with `2#2`, the last Friday with `5L`). It keeps the zone and the daylight-saving rules every schedule has, fires at most every five minutes, and the Routines tab and the approval card say it in words, never as the raw fields. A session automation does not take one; its editor has no field for it.
