Every control the platform has is the platform's: `Switch` and `Checkbox` are `@expo/ui` (SwiftUI on iOS, Material 3 on Android), `SegmentedControl` is new, and the JavaScript look-alikes (dialogs, popovers, dropdown and context menus, tabs, toggle and radio groups, the @gorhom bottom sheet) are removed. An automation's pause switch is Material 3's on Android.
