Bots react on Slack and WhatsApp: the `react` tool, which refused outside the console chat, now puts the bot's chosen emoji on the owner's message that opened its turn, on Slack as the bot's app (by Slack's name for it; 👀 stays the working sign) and on WhatsApp as the number, over the new `whatsapp.react` frame (capability `whatsapp.react`), spending the host's send budget.
