The confirmed owner's messages to their linked WhatsApp number wake the bot that answers the chat: one turn per stored message, in that chat's own conversation, however often it is delivered. A switch by name opens the named bot's conversation at the switch, `/new` starts a fresh one and `/stop` stops the chat's running turn. A bot the number's owner did not make is woken by nothing.
