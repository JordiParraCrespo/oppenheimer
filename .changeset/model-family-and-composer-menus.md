The composer's engine button offers the Claude family by name — Claude Fable
5.1, Claude Opus 5, Claude Sonnet 5, Claude Haiku 4.5, each row carrying the
full model name rather than a moving alias — and its two menus are back on the
export's own measurements: a 296px permission menu of 5px rows, a 252px engine
pane of 6px rows with a full-bleed search row and a list sized in rows. The
scope pickers take `loading`, so a chip whose list has not arrived says so
instead of greying itself out.
